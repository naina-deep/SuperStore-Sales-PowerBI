# SuperStore-Sales-PowerBI
Interactive SuperStore Sales analysis and dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to explore sales, profit, customer, product, and regional performance.

 # 🛒 SuperStore Sales Analysis — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing SuperStore sales data using **Microsoft Power BI**. The objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights through data analysis, interactive visualizations, and dashboard design.

The dashboard explores sales and profitability across different products, categories, customers, regions, and time periods to provide a clear overview of business performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance
* Identify high-performing product categories and sub-categories
* Analyze sales across different regions and locations
* Understand customer segment performance
* Identify sales and profit trends over time
* Analyze quantity and order performance
* Create interactive dashboards and visualizations
* Present data-driven insights in a clear and user-friendly format

## 🗂️ Dataset

The project uses the **SuperStore Sales Dataset**, containing transactional information related to orders, customers, products, locations, sales, quantity, profit, shipping, and payment methods.

## 🔍 Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

1. Which categories and sub-categories generate the highest sales?
2. Which products contribute the most to profitability?
3. Which regions and locations perform best?
4. Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
5. How do sales and profit change over time?
6. Which products have high sales but relatively low profitability?
7. Which shipping and payment methods are most commonly used?
8. Where are the strongest and weakest-performing markets?
